# Make your own interactive ShopMy closet

Your ShopMy shelf can look like a clean, bright closet. Your real product photos hang on gold hangers and sit on shelves. People tap a piece and land on that exact product, through your tracked ShopMy link, so your clicks and commission still count.

You do not need to know how to code.

## 1. Open Claude (or Codex or Claude Code) and start a new chat

This is where you ask it to build your site. Give it the guide link. You do not download or edit any code.

## 2. Copy and paste this

> Help me build my own interactive ShopMy closet using this guide: [INSERT GUIDE LINK]. Ask me for my name, my exact ShopMy shelf link, and my design preferences. Pull every product and its tracked link from my shelf. Tell me which product photos I need to send. Show me a plan and a mockup first, on a phone and on desktop. Let me choose where each item goes. After I approve, keep the design exactly as approved.

## 3. Answer its questions and make it yours

- Send your shelf link.
- Attach your product photos. Flat or hanging photos for clothes. Front view on white for shoes, bags, glasses and bottles. Photos with a model in them do not cut out well, so it will ask for better ones.
- Tell it the look you want. Examples: bright and clean, warm wood, gold hangers, editorial.
- Give feedback in plain words, like:
  - "Move the sunglasses to the top left box and center them."
  - "Make the necklace stand out more."
  - "Make the font brown, not red brown."
  - "Show me how it looks on my phone."

## 4. Ask to put it online

> Save my finished site in a new GitHub repo in my account, connect it to a new Vercel project, and publish it. Walk me through every sign-in step. Keep my approved design and my links exactly as they are. Check that every item opens the right product on phone and desktop. Give me the public link when it is live.

GitHub stores the files. Vercel puts them online. After they are connected, changes saved to GitHub update the same link.

Put the link in your bio.
