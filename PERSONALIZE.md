# Make it yours

Start with your own style and your own products. You do not need anyone else's layout.

## Pick a look

| Choice | Ideas |
| --- | --- |
| Scene | Closet, storefront window, suitcase, vanity, desk |
| Mood | Bright and clean, warm wood, boutique, editorial |
| Details | Gold hangers, shoes on the bottom shelf, accessories on top shelves |
| Things to leave out | Props you do not want (pumpkins, plants, candles) |
| Fonts | An elegant serif for your name, a simple sans for the rest |
| Color | Pick one accent, like coffee brown or sage |

Bring two or three inspiration photos. Tell your AI what you like about each: the light, the colors, the shelves, the hangers.

## Phone first

Most people will open this from Instagram on a phone. Ask for a tall, vertical scene so the whole closet fits on a phone screen with no sideways scrolling. On a computer it can show the closet on the left and your item list on the right.

## Photos to gather

| Item type | Best photo |
| --- | --- |
| Clothes | Flat lay or on a hanger, front view |
| Shoes | Side view on white |
| Bags, glasses, bottles | Front view on white |
| Jewelry | Close up on white |
| Beauty items | Front view on white |

Save product photos from the retailer page or your own camera roll and attach them in the chat. A photo of a model wearing it works only as a last choice.

## Item worksheet

Copy this for each item.

```text
Item name:
Brand:
Exact link from my shelf (or "pull it from my shelf"):
Photo attached (yes or no):
Where it should go (shelf, rail, left, middle, right):
Anything to avoid:
```

## Feedback you can copy

> Show me two layouts before you build.

> Keep the approved design. Change only the necklace.

> The cream jacket disappears on the white wall. Fix the contrast.

> Move the clip and lip gloss to the middle shelf, side by side, centered.

> Show me phone and desktop previews.
